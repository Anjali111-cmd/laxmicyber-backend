About CyberGuard AI

CyberGuard AI is a smart cyber complaint registration system designed to help users report and track cyber crimes efficiently. It leverages AI for complaint classification, provides a voice assistant for ease of use, and allows users to upload supporting evidence.

Our goal is to make the process of reporting cyber crimes simple, fast, and effective, ensuring that users can get the help they need while keeping their information secure.
